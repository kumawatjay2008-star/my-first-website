Our Story

A heritage home in the city of Mahakal

Ujjain has welcomed pilgrims and travellers for thousands of years. Om Shree Heritage carries that spirit forward — traditional warmth, comfortable rooms, and a stay that feels like coming home after darshan.

Whether you are here for Mahakaleshwar, the Kshipra ghats, or a family trip, our team looks after you the way guests have always been looked after: Atithi Devo Bhava.

Around You

Explore sacred Ujjain

One of India's oldest living cities — stay close to its most beloved places.

🕉️

Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas — the spiritual heart of Ujjain, famous for its Bhasma Aarti.

🌊

Ram Ghat · Kshipra

Evening aarti, diyas on the river & a Simhastha Kumbh legacy.

🔥

Kal Bhairav Mandir

An ancient temple with a one-of-a-kind tradition.

🛕

Harsiddhi Temple

A revered Shakti Peeth with glowing deepmalas.

📚

Sandipani Ashram

Where Krishna and Balram are said to have studied.

🔭

Vedh Shala

An old astronomical observatory — Ujjain was once the Prime Meridian of India.
